## Best Portable Projectors for iPhone & Android: Take Your Entertainment Anywhere

Portable projectors have become a must-have accessory for smartphone users who want to enjoy movies, presentations, or gaming on a bigger screen wherever they go. Whether you’re camping, hosting a backyard movie night, or giving a business presentation, these compact devices can transform your iPhone or Android phone into a portable entertainment hub. Here are some of the best portable projectors that pair seamlessly with your smartphone.

---

## Anker Nebula Capsule II

The Anker Nebula Capsule II is a popular choice for its compact design and impressive performance. This soda-can-sized projector delivers 720p HD resolution and supports up to 200 lumens of brightness, making it suitable for dimly lit environments. It runs on Android TV, so you can stream directly from apps like Netflix, YouTube, and Disney+ without needing your phone. However, it also supports screen mirroring via HDMI or Wi-Fi, making it compatible with both iPhone and Android devices.

Key Features:  
- Built-in Android TV for standalone streaming  
- 720p HD resolution  
- Compact and portable design  
- Up to 2.5 hours of battery life  

---

## XGIMI MoGo Pro

The XGIMI MoGo Pro is a step up in terms of brightness and resolution. It offers 1080p Full HD resolution and 300 lumens, ensuring clearer and brighter images even in slightly lit rooms. This projector also runs on Android TV, giving you access to a wide range of apps. The built-in Harman Kardon speakers deliver high-quality audio, eliminating the need for external speakers.

Key Features:  
- 1080p Full HD resolution  
- Android TV integration  
- Harman Kardon speakers  
- Compact and sleek design

## What to Look for in a Portable Projector

When choosing a portable projector for your iPhone or Android phone, consider the following factors:

### Resolution  
Opt for at least 720p HD resolution for clear and sharp images. If you want more detail, look for 1080p Full HD projectors.

### Brightness  
Brightness is measured in lumens. For indoor use, 200-500 lumens is sufficient, but for brighter environments, consider projectors with 500 lumens or more.

### Portability  
Check the size, weight, and battery life. If you plan to travel frequently, prioritize compact and lightweight models with long battery life.

### Connectivity  
Ensure the projector supports HDMI, USB-C, or wireless casting for seamless connectivity with your smartphone.

### Built-in Features  
Built-in speakers, Android TV integration, and power bank functionality can enhance your experience without requiring additional accessories.
