## Top 5 Budget-Friendly Smartwatches Compatible with Both iOS & Android  

Smartwatches have become essential companions for smartphone users, offering fitness tracking, notifications, and even mobile payments. However, finding an affordable model that works seamlessly with both **iOS and Android** can be tricky. Below, we’ve rounded up the **top 5 budget-friendly smartwatches** that deliver great features without breaking the bank.  

### What to Look for in a Budget Smartwatch  

Before diving into our picks, here’s what to consider when shopping for a budget-friendly smartwatch:  

- **Compatibility**: Ensure the watch works with **both iOS and Android** (some have limited features on one OS).  
- **Battery Life**: Look for at least **2-3 days** of battery to avoid daily charging.  
- **Health Tracking**: Basic sensors like **heart rate monitoring, step counting, and sleep tracking** are must-haves.  
- **Notifications**: Call, text, and app alerts should sync reliably.  
- **Build Quality**: A durable design (water resistance is a plus) ensures longevity.  

Now, let’s explore the best options under $200.  

---

## 1. **Amazfit Bip U Pro**  
**Price:** ~$70  

### Why It’s Great:  
The **Amazfit Bip U Pro** is one of the most affordable **cross-platform** smartwatches with impressive features. It runs on **Zepp OS** (compatible with iOS and Android) and offers:  

- **1.43" color touchscreen** with always-on display option  
- **SpO2 (blood oxygen) monitoring**, heart rate tracking, and sleep analysis  
- **Built-in GPS** for outdoor workouts  
- **5ATM water resistance** (swim-proof)  
- **Up to 9-day battery life**  

### Best For:  
Fitness enthusiasts who want **long battery life and GPS tracking** without spending much.  

---

## 2. **TicWatch E3 (by Mobvoi)**  
**Price:** ~$150  

### Why It’s Great:  
The **TicWatch E3** is one of the few budget-friendly **Wear OS** watches, meaning it supports **Google Assistant, Google Pay, and third-party apps** on both iOS and Android. Key features:  

- **Snapdragon Wear 4100 chip** for smooth performance  
- **IP68 water resistance** and MIL-STD-810G durability  
- **Heart rate, SpO2, and sleep tracking**  
- **Built-in GPS** for runs and cycling  
- **2-day battery life** (with essential usage)  

### Best For:  
Android users who want **Wear OS flexibility**, but it also works well with iPhones (though some features are limited).  

---

## 3. **Fitbit Versa 2**  
**Price:** ~$130 (refurbished or on sale)  

### Why It’s Great:  
The **Fitbit Versa 2** is a **fitness-focused** smartwatch with strong **iOS and Android** compatibility. While newer models exist, the Versa 2 remains a great budget pick:  

- **AMOLED display** with always-on option  
- **Alexa built-in** (no Google Assistant)  
- **24/7 heart rate tracking, sleep stages, and SpO2**  
- **5+ day battery life**  
- **Fitbit Pay** (NFC payments)  

### Best For:  
Users who prioritize **health metrics and long battery life** over app support.  

---

## 4. **Xiaomi Mi Watch Lite**  
**Price:** ~$80  

### Why It’s Great:  
Xiaomi’s **Mi Watch Lite** is a **simple, lightweight** smartwatch with solid cross-platform support. Features include:  

- **1.4" color touchscreen**  
- **117 workout modes** and **GPS tracking**  
- **Heart rate and sleep monitoring**  
- **5ATM water resistance**  
- **9-day battery life**  

### Best For:  
Budget-conscious buyers who want **basic fitness tracking** without extra frills.  

---

## 5. **Samsung Galaxy Watch 4 (Refurbished/Used)**  
**Price:** ~$150 (used/refurbished)  

### Why It’s Great:  
While the **Galaxy Watch 4** originally launched at a higher price, refurbished models now fit a tight budget. It runs **Wear OS** (with Samsung’s One UI) and offers:  

- **Super AMOLED display**  
- **Advanced health tracking** (ECG, BIA body composition)  
- **Google Assistant & Samsung Pay**  
- **IP68 + 5ATM water resistance**  
- **1.5-2 day battery life**  

*Note:* **iOS compatibility is limited** (no ECG/BIA on iPhone).  

### Best For:  
Android users who want **premium features at a discount**, but iPhone users may miss some functions.  

---

## Final Thoughts  

You don’t need to spend a fortune for a **reliable smartwatch** that works with **both iPhone and Android**. The **Amazfit Bip U Pro** is the best all-around budget pick, while the **TicWatch E3** excels for **Wear OS fans**. If **health tracking** is your priority, the **Fitbit Versa 2** or **Galaxy Watch 4** (refurbished) are great choices.  

Whichever you choose, prioritize **battery life, compatibility, and the features you’ll actually use** to get the best value. Happy tracking!
