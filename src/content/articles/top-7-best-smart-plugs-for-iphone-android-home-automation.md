# Top 7 Best Smart Plugs for iPhone & Android Home Automation  

Smart plugs are an easy way to automate your home without expensive rewiring. They let you control appliances remotely via smartphone, set schedules, and even monitor energy usage. Whether you use an iPhone or Android, compatibility is key—so we’ve rounded up the best smart plugs that work seamlessly with both platforms.  

## What to Look for in a Smart Plug  

Before diving into recommendations, consider these factors:  

- **Compatibility**: Ensure the plug works with Apple HomeKit (for iPhone) and/or Google Home/Alexa (for Android).  
- **Connectivity**: Wi-Fi plugs require no hub but depend on your network. Zigbee/Z-Wave plugs need a hub but offer better stability.  
- **Energy Monitoring**: Some plugs track power usage—handy for cutting costs.  
- **Design**: Compact plugs fit better in tight outlets. Outdoor-rated models are weatherproof.  
- **Voice Control**: Works with Siri, Google Assistant, or Alexa for hands-free use.  

Now, let’s explore the top picks.  

---

## 1. **TP-Link Kasa Smart Wi-Fi Plug Mini**  

**Best for:** Easy setup and energy monitoring  

- **Works with:** iPhone (HomeKit via bridge), Android (Google Home, Alexa)  
- **Features:** Compact design, energy usage tracking, scheduling, away mode  
- **Pros:** No hub required, reliable Wi-Fi connectivity  
- **Cons:** HomeKit requires separate hub  

Ideal for beginners, the Kasa Mini plugs into tight spaces and offers detailed energy reports via the Kasa app.  

---

## 2. **Wemo Mini Smart Plug**  

**Best for:** Apple HomeKit users  

- **Works with:** iPhone (HomeKit), Android (Google Home, Alexa)  
- **Features:** Thread support (for faster response), scheduling, sunset/sunrise automation  
- **Pros:** Native HomeKit support, compact size  
- **Cons:** No energy monitoring  

The Wemo Mini is a solid choice for iPhone users who want seamless Siri control without extra hardware.  

---

## 3. **Amazon Smart Plug**  

**Best for:** Alexa households  

- **Works with:** Android (Alexa), iPhone (via Alexa app)  
- **Features:** Voice control, simple setup, scheduling  
- **Pros:** Affordable, integrates with Alexa routines  
- **Cons:** No energy monitoring, limited to Alexa  

If you’re deep into the Amazon ecosystem, this plug is a no-brainer for basic automation.  

---

## 4. **Gosund Mini Wi-Fi Smart Plug**  

**Best for:** Budget-conscious buyers  

- **Works with:** iPhone & Android (Google Home, Alexa, Smart Life app)  
- **Features:** Energy monitoring (select models), timer/schedule, group control  
- **Pros:** Affordable, compact, works with multiple ecosystems  
- **Cons:** App can be clunky  

A wallet-friendly option that still delivers core smart features.  

---

## 5. **Eve Energy Smart Plug**  

**Best for:** Advanced HomeKit automation  

- **Works with:** iPhone (HomeKit), Android (limited via Eve app)  
- **Features:** Thread support, energy monitoring, Matter-compatible  
- **Pros:** Ultra-reliable, detailed power stats  
- **Cons:** Expensive, Android functionality is limited  

Eve Energy excels in Apple’s ecosystem with Thread for faster responses and Matter support for future-proofing.  

---

## 6. **Wyze Plug**  

**Best for:** Android users on a budget  

- **Works with:** Android (Google Home, Alexa), iPhone (via Wyze app)  
- **Features:** Energy monitoring, scheduling, away mode  
- **Pros:** Cheap, works with Wyze ecosystem  
- **Cons:** Requires Wyze app for full features  

Wyze Plug is a steal for Android users who want energy tracking without breaking the bank.  

---

## 7. **Innr Zigbee Smart Plug**  

**Best for:** Hub-based smart homes  

- **Works with:** iPhone/Android (via Zigbee hubs like SmartThings or Hue)  
- **Features:** Energy monitoring, compact design, works offline  
- **Pros:** Stable Zigbee connection, no Wi-Fi dependency  
- **Cons:** Requires a compatible hub  

If you already use a Zigbee hub (e.g., SmartThings), this plug offers rock-solid reliability.  

---

## Final Thoughts  

The best smart plug depends on your ecosystem and needs. For iPhone users, **Wemo Mini** or **Eve Energy** are top-tier. Android users will love **Wyze Plug** or **Gosund Mini**. If you want voice control, **Amazon Smart Plug** is Alexa’s best friend, while **TP-Link Kasa** balances features and affordability.  

Upgrade your home automation today—just plug in and go smart!
