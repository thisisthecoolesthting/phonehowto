## How to Block Spam Calls on Android: A Definitive Guide  

Spam calls are more than just annoying—they can be dangerous, exposing you to scams, phishing attempts, and fraud. Fortunately, Android offers several built-in and third-party solutions to block unwanted calls. In this guide, we’ll walk you through the best methods to stop spam calls on your Android device, including carrier services, built-in features, and recommended apps.  

### Why You Should Block Spam Calls  

Spam calls aren’t just a nuisance; they can lead to:  
- **Financial scams** (e.g., fake IRS calls, tech support scams)  
- **Identity theft** (phishing attempts asking for personal info)  
- **Disrupted productivity** (constant interruptions)  

By proactively blocking spam calls, you protect your privacy and peace of mind.  

### Method 1: Use Your Carrier’s Spam Protection  

Many carriers offer free spam-blocking services. Here’s how to enable them:  

#### **T-Mobile (Scam Shield)**  
- Open the **Scam Shield** app (pre-installed on T-Mobile devices).  
- Enable **Scam Block** and **Caller ID**.  
- Upgrade to **Scam Shield Premium** ($4/month) for advanced features like reverse number lookup.  

#### **Verizon (Call Filter)**  
- Download **Call Filter** from the Google Play Store.  
- Enable **Spam Detection** and **Caller ID**.  
- Paid plans ($2.99/month) add spam risk meters and custom block lists.  

#### **AT&T (Call Protect)**  
- Install **AT&T Call Protect** from the Play Store.  
- Turn on **Automatic Fraud Blocking** and **Spam Risk Alerts**.  

**What to Look For:**  
- Free vs. paid tiers (some features require subscriptions).  
- Real-time blocking vs. post-call alerts.  

### Method 2: Enable Android’s Built-in Spam Protection  

Most modern Android phones (running Android 9+) include spam-blocking features:  

1. **Open the Phone app** (Google Dialer or manufacturer variant).  
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** → **Settings** → **Spam and Call Screen**.  
3. Enable **Caller ID & Spam Protection** (Google Dialer) or **Block Spam Calls** (Samsung).  

**Note:** Samsung Galaxy devices have **Smart Call** (identifies and blocks suspected spam).  

### Method 3: Use Third-Party Call-Blocking Apps  

If your carrier or built-in options aren’t enough, these apps add extra protection:  

#### **Truecaller**  
- **Features:** Crowdsourced spam database, caller ID, SMS filtering.  
- **Free tier:** Basic blocking; **Premium ($7.99/month)** adds automatic blocking and no ads.  

#### **Hiya**  
- **Features:** Real-time spam detection, neighborhood spoofing alerts.  
- **Free tier:** Basic blocking; **Premium ($2.99/month)** offers caller profiles.  

#### **RoboKiller**  
- **Features:** AI-powered blocking, answer bots to waste scammers’ time.  
- **Subscription:** $4.99/month (7-day free trial).  

**What to Look For:**  
- Database size (bigger = better spam detection).  
- Privacy policies (some apps collect call data).  
- Battery impact (lightweight apps perform better).  

### Method 4: Manually Block Numbers  

For persistent spam numbers:  
1. Open the **Phone app** → **Recent Calls**.  
2. Tap the spam number → **Block/Report as spam**.  

**Tip:** On Samsung devices, you can also add numbers to a **Block List** in **Settings > Block Numbers**.  

### Method 5: Silence Unknown Callers  

If you only want calls from contacts:  
1. Go to **Settings > Sound & Vibration > Do Not Disturb**.  
2. Enable **Do Not Disturb** and customize exceptions (e.g., allow calls from contacts).  

**Downside:** May block legitimate calls from new businesses or doctors.  

### Method 6: Use Call Screening (Google Pixel Feature)  

Google Pixel’s **Call Screen** lets your assistant answer unknown calls:  
1. Open the **Phone app** → **Settings > Spam and Call Screen**.  
2. Enable **Call Screen** for suspected spam.  

**How It Works:** Google Assistant answers and transcribes the call in real time, so you can decide whether to pick up.  

### Extra Tips to Reduce Spam Calls  

- **Avoid answering unknown numbers** (even pressing a button can confirm your number is active).  
- **Don’t share your number publicly** (e.g., online forms, social media).  
- **Register with the National Do Not Call Registry** (U.S. only; reduces legal telemarketing calls).  

### Recommended Accessories for Call Management  

1. **Bluetooth Headphones with Call Controls** (e.g., **Jabra Elite 7 Pro**) – Quickly reject spam calls with a button press.  
2. **Smartwatches with Call Filtering** (e.g., **Samsung Galaxy Watch 5**) – Screen calls from your wrist.  

### Final Thoughts  

Blocking spam calls on Android is easier than ever, thanks to carrier tools, built-in features, and third-party apps. For best results, combine multiple methods (e.g., carrier protection + Truecaller). Stay vigilant, and don’t hesitate to block suspicious numbers manually.  

By following these steps, you’ll drastically reduce unwanted calls and enjoy a more peaceful smartphone experience.
