## Best Noise-Cancelling Headphones for iPhone & Android: Our Top 7 Picks  

Noise-cancelling headphones are a game-changer for smartphone users, whether you're commuting, working, or just trying to unwind. With so many options available, it can be tough to choose the right pair. Below, we’ve rounded up the **best noise-cancelling headphones** compatible with both **iPhone and Android**, highlighting key features to help you decide.  

---

## What to Look for in Noise-Cancelling Headphones  

Before diving into our picks, here’s what to consider when shopping:  

- **Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) Quality**: Look for headphones with strong ANC performance, especially for low-frequency sounds like airplane engines or traffic.  
- **Battery Life**: Longer battery life (20+ hours) is ideal for frequent travelers.  
- **Comfort & Fit**: Over-ear designs usually offer better noise isolation, while in-ear models are more portable.  
- **Sound Quality**: Balanced audio with clear mids and highs is crucial for music and calls.  
- **Smart Features**: Multipoint Bluetooth, voice assistants (Siri/Google Assistant), and app customization can enhance usability.  
- **Compatibility**: Ensure seamless pairing with both **iOS and Android** devices.  

Now, let’s explore our top recommendations.  

---

## 1. Sony WH-1000XM5  

**Best Overall for iPhone & Android**  

The **Sony WH-1000XM5** is a top-tier choice, offering industry-leading ANC, superb sound, and long battery life (up to 30 hours). Key features:  

- **Adaptive ANC** adjusts to your surroundings automatically.  
- **LDAC support** for high-resolution audio (Android-only, due to Apple’s AAC codec).  
- **Speak-to-Chat** pauses music when you talk.  
- **Comfortable over-ear design** with plush ear cushions.  

Works seamlessly with **iPhone (AAC) and Android (LDAC/AAC)**.  

---

## 2. Bose QuietComfort Ultra  

**Best for Comfort & Noise Isolation**  

Bose’s **QuietComfort Ultra** excels in comfort and ANC performance, making it perfect for long flights or work sessions:  

- **CustomTune technology** tailors sound and ANC to your ears.  
- **Immersive Audio** for spatial sound (works with both iOS and Android).  
- **24-hour battery life** with quick charging.  
- **Lightweight design** with deep ear cushions.  

A great all-rounder for **iPhone and Android users**.  

---

## 3. Apple AirPods Max  

**Best for iPhone Users (Limited Android Features)**  

If you’re deep in the Apple ecosystem, the **AirPods Max** deliver premium ANC and sound:  

- **H1 chip** enables effortless pairing with iPhones and instant switching between Apple devices.  
- **Transparency Mode** lets outside sound in when needed.  
- **Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking** (best with Apple Music/Dolby Atmos).  
- **20-hour battery life** (shorter than competitors).  

**Android users** miss out on some features (e.g., seamless pairing, Spatial Audio).  

---

## 4. Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless  

**Best for Audiophiles**  

The **Sennheiser Momentum 4 Wireless** offers exceptional sound quality with strong ANC:  

- **60-hour battery life** (one of the longest in its class).  
- **Adaptive ANC** and **Transparent Hearing** mode.  
- **EQ customization** via the Smart Control app (iOS/Android).  
- **Sleek, minimalist design** with comfortable fit.  

A great pick for **both platforms**, especially if sound clarity is a priority.  

---

## 5. Bose QuietComfort Earbuds II  

**Best True Wireless for ANC**  

For those who prefer earbuds, the **Bose QC Earbuds II** pack powerful ANC in a compact form:  

- **Customizable ANC** via the Bose Music app (iOS/Android).  
- **Secure Fit Kit** ensures a snug fit for better noise isolation.  
- **6-hour battery** (18+ with case).  
- **IPX4 water resistance** for workouts.  

Works well with **iPhone and Android**, though slightly bulkier than AirPods Pro.  

---

## 6. Jabra Elite 85t  

**Best for Calls & Multipoint Connectivity**  

The **Jabra Elite 85t** is a strong contender for business users and frequent callers:  

- **Semi-open design** reduces ear pressure while maintaining solid ANC.  
- **6-mic setup** with advanced call clarity.  
- **Multipoint Bluetooth** (connect to two devices at once).  
- **5.5-hour battery** (25+ with case).  

Ideal for **Android and iPhone users** who need reliable call quality.  

---

## 7. Anker Soundcore Space Q45  

**Best Budget Option**  

If you want solid ANC without breaking the bank, the **Soundcore Space Q45** delivers:  

- **Up to 50-hour battery life** (ANC on).  
- **LDAC support** (Android only) for better audio quality.  
- **Customizable ANC modes** via app.  
- **Lightweight and comfortable** for long wear.  

A great budget-friendly choice for **both platforms**.  

---

## Final Thoughts  

Whether you prioritize **ANC performance, sound quality, or budget**, there’s a perfect pair for your **iPhone or Android** device. The **Sony WH-1000XM5** is our top pick for its versatility, while the **AirPods Max** shine for Apple loyalists. For earbuds, the **Bose QC Earbuds II** and **Jabra Elite 85t** are excellent options.  

Choose based on your needs—happy listening!
