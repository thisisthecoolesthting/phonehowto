# Top 10 Best Photo Editing Apps for iPhone and Android  

Whether you're a casual photographer or a social media enthusiast, having the right photo editing app can transform your smartphone snaps into stunning visuals. Below, we’ve compiled a list of the **10 best photo editing apps** for iPhone and Android, highlighting their key features, strengths, and ideal use cases.  

## 1. Adobe Lightroom Mobile  
**Best for:** Professional-grade editing & RAW support  
Adobe Lightroom Mobile is a powerhouse for serious photographers. It offers **non-destructive editing**, RAW file support, and advanced tools like **curves, split toning, and selective adjustments**. The app syncs seamlessly with the desktop version, making it perfect for those who edit across devices.  

**Key Features:**  
- Presets for quick edits  
- Healing brush for spot removal  
- Cloud storage for syncing edits  

## 2. Snapseed (by Google)  
**Best for:** Free, feature-packed editing  
Snapseed remains one of the best **free photo editing apps**, offering professional tools without a subscription. Its **selective adjustment brush** and **HDR Scape filter** help enhance details in shadows and highlights.  

**Key Features:**  
- 29 tools and filters  
- Selective editing with brushes  
- No watermarks or ads  

## 3. VSCO  
**Best for:** Film-like filters & minimalist editing  
VSCO is beloved for its **film-inspired presets** and clean interface. While it offers basic editing tools, its strength lies in its **signature filters** that emulate classic film stocks.  

**Key Features:**  
- 200+ premium presets (some paid)  
- Advanced camera controls  
- Community sharing (optional)  

## 4. PicsArt  
**Best for:** Creative collages & AI tools  
PicsArt is a versatile app for **collages, stickers, and AI-powered effects**. It’s great for social media creators who want to add artistic flair to their photos.  

**Key Features:**  
- Cutout tool for removing backgrounds  
- AI-powered effects (e.g., cartoonizer)  
- Sticker and text overlays  

## 5. Afterlight  
**Best for:** Vintage & texture effects  
Afterlight excels in **film grain, light leaks, and double exposures**, making it ideal for retro-style edits. The app includes **over 130 textures and 60+ filters**.  

**Key Features:**  
- Advanced blending modes  
- Dust & scratch overlays for vintage looks  
- Curves and color balance tools  

## 6. Facetune (by Lightricks)  
**Best for:** Portrait retouching  
Facetune is a go-to for **selfie and portrait editing**, offering tools like **skin smoothing, teeth whitening, and reshaping**. It’s great for influencers and anyone who wants polished portraits.  

**Key Features:**  
- AI-powered skin retouching  
- Background replacement tool  
- Detail enhancer for eyes/hair  

## 7. Foodie (by LINE Corporation)  
**Best for:** Food photography  
Foodie is designed specifically for **enhancing food photos** with filters that make dishes look more vibrant and appetizing.  

**Key Features:**  
- Food-specific filters (e.g., "Yum," "Fresh")  
- Blur effects for background bokeh  
- Easy brightness/contrast adjustments  

## 8. TouchRetouch  
**Best for:** Object removal & quick fixes  
TouchRetouch specializes in **removing unwanted objects** from photos, like power lines, photobombers, or blemishes.  

**Key Features:**  
- One-tap object removal  
- Clone stamp tool  
- Line removal for straight edges  

## 9. Canva  
**Best for:** Social media graphics & templates  
Canva isn’t just for photo editing—it’s perfect for **creating Instagram posts, stories, and marketing materials** with pre-sized templates.  

**Key Features:**  
- Drag-and-drop design tools  
- Thousands of free templates  
- Built-in stock photo library  

## 10. Darkroom  
**Best for:** Batch editing & color grading  
Darkroom offers **batch editing** and **advanced color grading**, making it efficient for editing multiple photos in the same style.  

**Key Features:**  
- Custom filter creation  
- Video editing support  
- Histogram for exposure control  

## What to Look for in a Photo Editing App  
When choosing the best photo editing app, consider:  
- **Editing Tools:** Does it have the features you need (e.g., curves, healing brush)?  
- **Ease of Use:** Is the interface intuitive?  
- **Export Quality:** Does it maintain high resolution?  
- **Cost:** Are there subscriptions or one-time purchases?  
- **Platform:** Is it available on both iPhone and Android?  

### Final Thoughts  
Whether you need **quick filters, professional adjustments, or creative effects**, there’s an app for every editing style. Try a few to see which fits your workflow best!
