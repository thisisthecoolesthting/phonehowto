## Top 8 Best Tripods for Smartphone Photography & Videography  

Whether you're capturing stunning landscapes, shooting smooth videos, or taking group selfies, a good tripod is essential for smartphone photography and videography. The right tripod stabilizes your shots, enables creative angles, and reduces shaky footage. Below, we’ve rounded up the **best tripods** for iPhone and Android users, covering different budgets and use cases.  

### What to Look for in a Smartphone Tripod  

Before diving into recommendations, here are key factors to consider:  

- **Stability & Build Quality**: Look for sturdy legs and a solid base to prevent wobbling.  
- **Height & Adjustability**: Choose between compact tabletop tripods or full-height models.  
- **Mount Compatibility**: Ensure the tripod includes a smartphone clamp or cold shoe for accessories.  
- **Portability**: Lightweight, foldable designs are ideal for travel.  
- **Extras**: Features like a built-in level, Bluetooth remote, or flexible legs add versatility.  

Now, let’s explore the best options available.  

---

## 1. Joby GorillaPod Mobile Rig  

**Best for:** Versatile shooting with accessories  

The **Joby GorillaPod Mobile Rig** is a favorite among mobile creators. Its flexible, grippy legs wrap around objects or stand freely, while the ball head allows 360° adjustments. The included cold shoe mount lets you attach microphones or lights, making it perfect for vloggers.  

**Key Features:**  
- Flexible, wrap-around legs  
- Ball head for precise angles  
- Cold shoe and 1/4" thread for accessories  
- Compact and travel-friendly  

---

## 2. Manfrotto PIXI Mini Tripod  

**Best for:** Compact stability  

The **Manfrotto PIXI** is a sturdy, pocket-sized tripod with a quick-release smartphone mount. Its rubberized legs provide grip on uneven surfaces, and the ball head tilts for portrait or landscape shots. Ideal for tabletop photography or quick setups.  

**Key Features:**  
- Durable aluminum build  
- Rubberized legs for grip  
- Quick-release mount  
- Weighs just 0.3 lbs  

---

## 3. Ulanzi ST-06 Smartphone Tripod  

**Best for:** Budget-friendly stability  

The **Ulanzi ST-06** offers excellent value with a full-height aluminum tripod and a secure smartphone clamp. It extends up to 60 inches, making it great for group shots or overhead filming. The included Bluetooth remote is a bonus for hands-free shooting.  

**Key Features:**  
- Extends to 60 inches  
- Lightweight aluminum legs  
- Comes with a Bluetooth remote  
- Affordable price  

---

## 4. Peak Design Mobile Tripod  

**Best for:** Premium design & portability  

The **Peak Design Mobile Tripod** is sleek, lightweight, and ultra-compact. It folds into a credit-card-sized form factor, making it perfect for travelers. The magnetic phone mount ensures quick attachment, and the legs adjust for low-angle shots.  

**Key Features:**  
- Ultra-compact folding design  
- Magnetic phone mount  
- Supports up to 3.3 lbs  
- Premium aluminum build

## 5. UBeesize 50" Extendable Tripod  

**Best for:** All-in-one kit  

The **UBeesize 50" Tripod** is a complete package, featuring a full-height tripod, ring light, and Bluetooth remote. The adjustable legs work on any surface, and the ring light is great for low-light selfies or video calls.  

**Key Features:**  
- Extends to 50 inches  
- Built-in ring light with brightness control  
- Includes Bluetooth remote  
- Rubber feet for stability

The **Benro BK-10** is a robust tripod with a fluid head for smooth panning in videos. It supports heavier setups, making it ideal for filmmakers using external mics or gimbals. The legs lock securely at multiple angles.  

**Key Features:**

## 8. Smatree Portable Tripod  

**Best for:** Outdoor & action shots  

The **Smatree Portable Tripod** is rugged, with non-slip feet and a waterproof phone mount. It’s great for outdoor adventures, and the flexible legs can attach to branches or rocks for unique angles.

## Final Thoughts  

Choosing the right tripod depends on your needs—whether it’s portability, stability, or extra features like a remote or ring light. For most users, the **Joby GorillaPod Mobile Rig** or **Ulanzi ST-06** offer the best balance of versatility and affordability. If you prioritize compactness, the **Peak Design Mobile Tripod** is unbeatable.  

Investing in a good tripod will elevate your smartphone photography and videography, ensuring sharper images and smoother videos. Happy shooting!
