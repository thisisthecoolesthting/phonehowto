## How to Scan Documents with Your iPhone or Android Camera  

Scanning documents with your smartphone is a quick and convenient way to digitize receipts, contracts, notes, and more. Both iPhone and Android devices have built-in tools for scanning, and third-party apps can enhance the experience. Here’s how to scan documents efficiently and what accessories can help improve your results.  

## Built-in Scanning Tools  

### Scanning with iPhone (Notes App)  
1. Open the **Notes** app and create a new note or open an existing one.  
2. Tap the **camera icon** and select **Scan Documents**.  
3. Position your iPhone over the document. The app will auto-detect edges—hold steady until the scan is captured.  
4. Adjust the corners manually if needed, then tap **Keep Scan**.  
5. Save the scan as a PDF or image within the note.  

**Pro Tip:** Enable **Auto** mode in the scanner settings for faster captures.  

### Scanning with Android (Google Drive or Files by Google)  
- **Google Drive Method:**  
  1. Open **Google Drive**, tap the **+** button, and select **Scan**.  
  2. Align the document in the frame. The app will auto-capture or let you manually snap the shot.  
  3. Adjust cropping, color filters (e.g., grayscale, color), and tap **Save**.  

- **Files by Google Method:**  
  1. Open **Files by Google**, go to **Browse**, and tap **Scan**.  
  2. Follow the on-screen prompts to capture and save the scan.  

Both methods save scans as PDFs to your Google Drive or device storage.  

## Third-Party Scanning Apps  

If you need advanced features like OCR (Optical Character Recognition), batch scanning, or cloud integrations, try these apps:  

- **Adobe Scan (iOS/Android):** Free, with OCR, cloud sync, and editing tools.  
- **Microsoft Lens (iOS/Android):** Great for whiteboard scans and Office integrations.  
- **CamScanner (iOS/Android):** Offers annotation and collaboration features (some features require a subscription).  

## Improving Scan Quality  

### Lighting and Positioning  
- Use **natural light** or a well-lit room to avoid shadows.  
- Place the document on a flat, contrasting surface (e.g., a dark table for white paper).  
- Hold the phone parallel to the document to reduce distortion.  

### Accessories to Enhance Scanning  
1. **Phone Stand or Tripod:** Keeps your device steady for multi-page scans. Look for adjustable stands like the **Joby GorillaPod**.  
2. **Portable Scanner Lights:** Clip-on LED lights (e.g., **Lume Cube**) improve low-light scans.  
3. **Document Holders:** A flat, weighted holder (like a book stand) prevents papers from curling.  

## Editing and Organizing Scans  
After scanning:  
- **Crop and Rotate:** Use built-in tools or apps like **Adobe Scan** to fine-tune edges.  
- **Color Adjustments:** Convert to black-and-white for text-heavy documents to reduce file size.  
- **File Naming:** Rename scans clearly (e.g., "Receipt_20231015.pdf") for easy retrieval.  

## What to Look for in a Scanning App or Accessory  
- **OCR Support:** Essential for converting scanned text into editable content.  
- **Cloud Integration:** Auto-sync to Google Drive, iCloud, or Dropbox saves time.  
- **Batch Scanning:** Useful for multi-page documents.  
- **Portability:** Foldable stands or pocket-sized lights are ideal for on-the-go scanning.  

## Final Tips  
- For sensitive documents, use apps with password protection (e.g., **Adobe Scan**).  
- Regularly clear old scans to free up storage.  
- Test multiple apps to find one that suits your workflow.  

With these tools and techniques, your smartphone can replace a bulky scanner for everyday use. Whether you’re archiving receipts or digitizing handwritten notes, a few taps are all it takes to go paperless.
