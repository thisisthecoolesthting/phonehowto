## iPhone FaceTime Tips: Hidden Features for Better Video Calls  

FaceTime is one of the most seamless video-calling apps for iPhone users, offering high-quality audio and video with deep iOS integration. But beyond the basics, there are several hidden features that can enhance your calls—whether for work, family chats, or virtual hangouts. Here’s how to get the most out of FaceTime.  

### 1. Use FaceTime on Non-Apple Devices  
While FaceTime is an Apple-exclusive app, you can still include Android or Windows users via a **FaceTime web link**. Here’s how:  
- Open the FaceTime app and tap **Create Link**.  
- Share the link via Messages, Email, or any other platform.  
- The recipient can join from their browser without needing an Apple ID.  

**Pro Tip:** For better compatibility, ensure the other person uses Chrome or Edge on a computer, as mobile browser support is limited.  

### 2. Enable Portrait Mode for a Cleaner Background  
Just like in the Camera app, FaceTime supports **Portrait Mode**, which blurs your background to keep the focus on you. To enable it:  
- Start a FaceTime call.  
- Swipe down from the top-right to open Control Center.  
- Tap the **Video Effects** button (the silhouette icon).  
- Toggle **Portrait** on.  

**Best for:** Professional calls or when your surroundings are cluttered.  

### 3. Share Your Screen for Collaboration  
Need to walk someone through a document or presentation? Use **Screen Sharing**:  
- During a call, tap the screen to reveal controls.  
- Tap the **Share Content** button (rectangle with arrow).  
- Select **Share My Screen**.  

**What to look for:** Ensure you’re on a stable Wi-Fi connection to avoid lag.  

### 4. Use Voice Isolation or Wide Spectrum Audio  
iOS offers advanced microphone modes to improve call clarity:  
- **Voice Isolation:** Reduces background noise (ideal for noisy environments).  
- **Wide Spectrum:** Picks up all ambient sounds (great for group discussions).  

To switch modes:  
- Open Control Center during a call.  
- Tap the **Mic Mode** button (microphone icon).  
- Select your preferred mode.  

### 5. Schedule Calls with FaceTime Links in Calendar  
Planning a group call? Integrate FaceTime with your calendar:  
- Create a FaceTime link (as described earlier).  
- Add it to a Calendar event and invite participants.  
- When it’s time, everyone can join with one tap.  

### 6. Try FaceTime with Apple TV for Big-Screen Calls  
If you have an **Apple TV 4K**, you can mirror FaceTime calls to your TV:  
- Start a FaceTime call on your iPhone.  
- Swipe into Control Center, tap **Screen Mirroring**, and select your Apple TV.  

**Best for:** Family calls where everyone wants to see the conversation on a larger display.  

### 7. Use Reactions (Like Zoom, but Better)  
Add fun reactions during calls with simple gestures:  
- 👍 Thumbs up = Double-tap with two fingers.  
- ❤️ Heart = Make a heart with your hands.  
- 🎉 Fireworks = Thumbs up with both hands.  

These appear as animated effects on-screen.  

### 8. Record FaceTime Calls (With Permission)  
Need to save a call for later? Use **Screen Recording**:  
- Enable **Screen Recording** in Control Center (Settings > Control Center > Add Screen Recording).  
- Start recording after confirming with participants (laws vary on consent).  

**Note:** The recording will include both video and audio.  

### 9. Switch Between Front and Rear Cameras Mid-Call  
Want to show something in your surroundings? Double-tap the screen during a call to switch cameras instantly.  

### 10. Use FaceTime Audio for High-Quality Calls  
If video isn’t necessary, **FaceTime Audio** offers superior call quality over traditional cellular calls:  
- Open FaceTime and tap the **+** button.  
- Enter a contact and select **Audio**.  

**Best for:** Long-distance calls where data is limited.  

### What to Look for in FaceTime Accessories  
To enhance your experience, consider these accessories:  
- **Lighting:** A ring light or softbox improves video quality in low light.  
- **Microphone:** External mics (like the Rode VideoMic Me-L) boost audio clarity.  
- **Tripod:** A phone stand or tripod keeps your frame steady.  
- **Headphones:** AirPods Pro (with noise cancellation) are ideal for clear audio.  

By mastering these hidden features, you’ll make your FaceTime calls more engaging, professional, and efficient. Whether for work or personal use, these tips ensure you’re getting the most out of Apple’s video-calling platform.
